William Boos is a Professor in the Department of Earth & Planetary Science at UC Berkeley. His research uses theory, observations, and numerical models to understand the atmospheric circulations, monsoons, and extreme weather that control the distribution of heat and water on Earth. He holds a Ph.D. in Atmospheric Science and an M.Sc. in Geosystems from MIT, and a B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Mathematics from SUNY Binghamton.
